## 1. Background

The Mission of the Institute for Social Science Research (ISSR) at the University of Alabama is to promote and support high-quality social science research across various disciplines. The ISSR aims to foster interdisciplinary collaboration, provide resources and support for social science research, and contribute to the advancement of knowledge and understanding of social phenomena.
